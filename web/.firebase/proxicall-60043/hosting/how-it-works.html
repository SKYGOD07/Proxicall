How ProxiCall Works

01Proximity Detection

The Agent constantly scans for your specific Bluetooth beacon (Smartwatch or Phone).

02Context Analysis

When a call comes in, ProxiCall checks your status: Are you driving? In a meeting? Sleeping?

03Gemini Decision

Google's Gemini 3 analyzes the caller ID and your context to decide the best action.

04Autonomous Action

The Agent either auto-replies via SMS, whispers a summary to your ear, or lets the call ring.
